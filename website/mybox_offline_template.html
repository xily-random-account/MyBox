MyBox Offline

MyBox works fully offline. All features are available without an internet connection.

All song data is stored directly in the URL. When you edit your song, the URL updates automatically. To save or share your song, simply copy the URL.

Instructions

You can add or remove notes by clicking on the gray rows at the top.

Notes belong to patterns. The numbered boxes at the bottom let you switch between patterns. Click a box to move to that part of the song, or use the arrows to swap patterns.

MyBox supports multiple pattern rows, including a drum row at the bottom.

The purple loop under the pattern boxes controls which part of the song repeats. Drag the ends to expand or shrink the loop.

Keyboard shortcuts:

  • Spacebar: play/pause
  • Shift Spacebar: play from mouse location
  • Z: undo   |   Y / Shift Z: redo
  • C/V: copy/paste patterns
  • 0–9: assign pattern number
  • Arrows: move selection
  • Ctrl + Arrows: rearrange channels
  • [ ]: move playhead
  • F/H: jump to First/Highlighted pattern
  • Shift + Drag: select part of a pattern

Long‑press in the pattern editor to select a time range.

Drag horizontally on a note to change duration. Drag vertically to bend pitch or adjust volume.

MyBox has many more features — explore the menus on the right side to discover them.

Offline Notes

This offline version contains everything needed to run MyBox without internet access. Your songs never leave your device unless you share the URL.
